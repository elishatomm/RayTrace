Matching rules. Direct: IDs and quoted spans verbatim between identifier boundaries, in the item's evidence ID, title, text, fact keys and string values, and the block the model received when it is stored. A number equals a fact or a number written in a text; with decimals it also matches a value that rounds to it at its stated precision; a percentage also matches a ratio from 0 to 1 at its stated precision. A clock time or date matches verbatim or a timestamp's UTC value. Loose, tried only when nothing matches directly: a span after lower-casing and treating spaces, underscores and hyphens alike; a number as the difference of two figures, or the total of one map, inside one item; a percentage as the difference of two ratios, in points. A match on a small integer, or a loose match, is weak evidence: 0, 1 and 2 occur in most items, and differences coincide.
